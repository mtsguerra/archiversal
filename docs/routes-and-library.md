# Routes and Library, in full

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` to keep it short. The short versions there are the summary; this is the detail.

- Routes (URL hash, no router, `frontend/src/route.js`): `#/` homepage (`components/home/Home.jsx`: the pencil-drawn title, then the three modes drawn as the levels of a building (`Levels`: Normal at +6.40, Themed +3.20 (links to `#/themed`), Custom ±0.00, a datum marker glides between them; the Daily is only reached from Today's sheet), Today's sheet (`DailyTeaser`, an "Approved" stamp once played), the Daily streak, the Library card (how many buildings are unlocked, a link to `#/library`) under it (same size, bottom aligned with the Archive; under the Archive on phones) and the style/country Archive; the layout is a 37×22 grid of 2.5rem squares, see `docs/design-log.md`), `#/play` Normal, `#/daily` Daily (date-seeded building, local midnight, result in localStorage; `#/daily/<n>` plays an earlier day from the "Other days" list in the result card (`summary/PastDays.jsx`): results are kept per day in `arch-guesser:daily-days`, a day played after its day is `late` (dashed marker in the streak card and the list) and never counts for the streak), `#/themed` theme index and `#/themed/play` session (20 themes of 14+ buildings each, a theme needs 10 to be listed; three difficulties: Casual name + era, Challenging + century, Custom (only the building is always asked; the player toggles era, century, decade, country, architect; a decade needs its century); 10 rounds; the index is a gallery of blurred photo tiles with a left menu (name search, theme groups, Random theme with rolling dice; it folds to a tab): on wide screens (1025px+) the chosen tile opens into a block of cells chosen by `blockLayout` so every row stays full (about 3×3), the photo spreading over it with a one-line description and the difficulty accordion as a glass card, tablets get a dock under the tiles, phones one list whose chosen row opens the difficulty (arrow keys move the radios); `game/themed.js`, `components/themed/`), `#/custom` setup and `#/custom/play` session (filters: architect, era, country, or country + era; at least 10 buildings; 3 to 50 rounds or all; from `GET /api/buildings/catalogue`; session in sessionStorage, config in localStorage, both sanitized on read). All render `Game.jsx` except the setup and homepage.

- Library (`#/library`, `components/library/`, `game/library.js` + `game/libraryView.js`): every building in the collection as a drafting-sheet register, locked until its reveal (any mode, solved or given up; naming the building adds the Mastered mark). Unlocks are stored per device in localStorage (`arch-guesser:library`: id → day, mode, mastered, attempts; sanitized on read; recorded by `useGameRound`, which also toasts when a reveal completes an era). Rows come from `GET /api/buildings/library?lang=` (static `library.json`, `pt-BR/library.json`). A locked row shows only its era and sheet number (A-101…, id order); a search or country filter never matches a locked building. Stats strip, search, group by era/century/country, era and country filters, Show (All/Unlocked/Mastered/Locked), era progress with the era's line drawing once complete, a globe (`game/globe.js`, shared with `VeiledGlobe`; zooms with +/− or a trackpad pinch) with a pin per country (the globe and the era progress are the side rail's two cards: on wide screens each folds like the Themed menu, and with both folded the rail narrows to a "Map and progress" tab), a panel per row (facts, neighbouring styles from `CLOSE_ERAS`, compare), a Compare dialog (two buildings, same scale) and "Play a locked one" (a one-building Custom session). An unlocked building opens at `#/library/<id>` (the reveal summary through `Round` with `replay={false}` and `fields` null; a locked id goes back to the Library). Buildings revealed before the Library existed are not in it.
